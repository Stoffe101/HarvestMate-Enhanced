# Changelog

## 1.0.1

### Fixed
- Glimmer colors now apply. Each glint's bright center takes on the chosen color instead of washing it out to white.
- A beast corpse no longer shows the skinning glimmer while it still has loot. It glimmers as an Other lootable until you loot it, then switches to the skinning glimmer. A corpse with loot you can't take does not glimmer.

### Added
- Skinnable beasts now have their own Glimmer row, with an on/off switch and a color (red by default).
- `/gp glimmer color skin <color>` and `/gp glimmer skin on|off`.

### Changed
- The Glimmer page has been rebuilt to match the Guides and Trainers tabs: a status card with the On/Off switch, a "What glimmers" card with each kind and its colors, and a "Look" card for the sliders, with more spacing throughout.
